<h2 align="center">🚀 About Me</h2>

<img align="right" src="./assets/astro.png" width="280" alt="Developer Illustration"/>

**Angel** here — a full-stack developer based in Barranquilla, Colombia, working with Node/TypeScript at **Gases del Caribe**.

My day-to-day is **NestJS 11** on the backend and **Next.js 15/16 + React 19** on the frontend, mostly inside **Turborepo** monorepos — plus keeping legacy **Express + Sequelize** services alive and healthy.

For data and infrastructure I work with **PostgreSQL**, **Redis/BullMQ** queues, and ship everything through **Docker**, **Helm** and **Kubernetes**.

On my own time I explore **Java / Spring Boot** and write **Pine Script v6** indicators for TradingView.

I'm a developer in constant growth: I care about understanding *why* something works, not just making it work.
